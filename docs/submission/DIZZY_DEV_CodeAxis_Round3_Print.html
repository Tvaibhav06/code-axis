1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

Brand Review Desk is an enterprise brand governance and campaign pre-flight review platform built in response to Inkloom AI's brand-governance brief. In modern marketing, multi-channel assets (ad copy, graphical banners, campaign briefs) are frequently deployed with outdated offer durations, off-brand claims, or conflicting terms. Brand Review Desk introduces an automated, explainable, and multi-modal verification loop that checks marketing materials before publication.

A non-negotiable architectural principle is that AI assists, but humans decide. The platform never automatically publishes campaigns; instead, it enforces an immutable approval transaction binding a specific campaign revision, published rulebook version, and check run. In our founding SampleCo scenario, draft copy claims a "30 days free trial" and "guaranteed 50% savings" while the approved brand rulebook specifies 14 days and prohibits unverified guarantees. The system flags the conflicts, quotes offending spans, references source facts, checks visual banner text, parses brief documents, and produces an exportable, self-contained HTML audit record.

3. Delivery of the Three Round 3 Commitments

In Round 2, DIZZY DEV committed to delivering three substantial capabilities during the final 72-hour window. All three have been fully implemented, verified, and hardened:

Improvement 1: Review Text Inside Images (OCR)

  • Engine & Scope: Integrated server-side OCR via Tesseract.js supporting still PNG and JPG images up to 5 MB and 4,096 pixels per side.
  • Human Confirmation: The creator clicks "Read text from this image," reviews the recognized text in an editable preview, and confirms it before claims enter the review pipeline.
  • Cross-Modal Verification: The engine flags multi-modal discrepancies where text copy and image text contradict each other (e.g., copy claims 30 days while image banner promises 14 days).
  • Acceptance Target: Achieved 10/10 passing fixtures on our benchmark test suite (exceeding the 8/10 target), with unreadable text properly routed to mandatory manual review.

Improvement 2: Review Short Text PDFs

  • Native Stream Parsing: Integrated server-side PDF text extraction via pdfjs-dist and pdf-parse, extracting per-page text maps (Page 1, Page 2).
  • Boundary Protection: Strictly enforces limits (up to 5 pages, 2 MB, 1,500 extracted words). Rejects scanned non-text PDFs, encrypted files, and oversized documents with actionable error messages.
  • Explainable Page References: Violations in supporting documents explicitly cite the exact page number (e.g., "Violates Rule on Page 2").
  • Full Workflow Integration: Extracted document text is saved in campaign versions and checked by both exact rules and AI checks alongside copy text.

Improvement 3: Import Guidelines with Manager Confirmation

  • Candidate Extraction: Reuses document parsing to propose up to 10 structured rule candidates (trial days, pricing, terminology, tone) from uploaded brand PDFs.
  • Anti-Hallucination Guard: Every candidate is programmatically verified as a verbatim substring of the PDF text, preventing injected or hallucinated rules.
  • Manager Verification: Managers can edit candidate titles/values, assign owners and validity dates, discard non-rules, and explicitly publish.
  • Approval Invalidation: Publishing a newly imported rulebook increments the database version, immediately invalidating prior campaign approvals.

4. Reliability, Resilience & Usability Hardening

A core evaluation criterion for Round 3 is product reliability without developer intervention. We resolved critical real-world failure modes:

  • Atomic Server-Side Uploads: Re-architected image and PDF uploads through /api/assets/upload and /api/assets/upload-pdf, completely eliminating browser-to-storage CORS preflight failures and ambient cookie serialization errors.
  • Trans-Continental Network Resilience: Diagnosed remote Supabase Auth latency (~11s) and expanded network timeouts from 10s to 30s across supabase.ts, proxy.ts, and demo.ts, eliminating premature 503 aborts.
  • Concurrency & Double-Click Locks: Added immediate synchronous busy locks on demo initialization, role switching, and version saves to eliminate race conditions.
  • Demo Pool Auto-Recycling: Added the recycle_expired_demo_slots migration function to reclaim expired demo workspaces, ensuring visitors never hit exhausted pool capacity.
  • Deterministic Fail-Open Architecture: If external AI calls fail or exhaust provider quotas, the application fails open cleanly to deterministic TypeScript rule checking and manual checklists. The user journey is never blocked.

7. Product Roadmap & Future Scope

The architectural foundation built in Round 3 positions Brand Review Desk for enterprise expansion:

Milestone 1: Multi-Tenant Enterprise Workspaces & Custom Brand Portals (Q1 2027)

  • Self-Service Company Instances: Allow organizations to register their own dedicated company workspaces with custom subdomains (e.g., review.brand.com) and enterprise SAML/SSO integration.
  • Multi-Campaign Architecture: Support multiple concurrent campaign tracks, product lines, and sub-brands under unified organizational guidelines.
  • Granular Role-Based Access: Introduce specialized roles for External Creative Agencies, Legal Directors, and Compliance Auditors.

Milestone 2: Enterprise AI Headroom & Multi-Model Resilience (Q2 2027)

  • Dedicated Enterprise AI Key & Higher Rate Limits: Provision dedicated enterprise-tier API keys (Vertex AI / Gemini Paid Tier) with guaranteed throughput SLAs and high concurrency headroom.
  • Multi-Model Fallback Mesh: Automatic failover across independent frontier models (Gemini 2.5 Flash → Claude 3.5 Sonnet → GPT-4o) when provider latency exceeds 5 seconds.
  • Adaptive Brand Learning: Fine-tune embeddings on historical reviewer feedback to catch subtle nuances in brand tone and style.

Milestone 3: Expanded Ingestion & Direct Marketing Integrations (Q3 2027)

  • DOCX & Creative Ingestion: Extend file ingestion to parse Microsoft Word (.docx) briefs and Figma design frames.
  • Direct Publishing Webhooks: Export cryptographically approved review bundles directly to CMS platforms (Contentful, WordPress) and ad networks (Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager) upon final approval.
  • Multilingual Compliance: Expand OCR and rule checks to multilingual global campaigns (Spanish, German, French, Hindi, Japanese).

8. Judge Walkthrough & Quick Verification Guide

  1. Access Demo: Open https://brand-review-desk.vercel.app/ and click "Start demo" (instantly leases an isolated workspace as Creator).
  2. Test Image OCR: Upload docs/manual-fixtures/sampleco-campaign.png, click "Read text from this image", verify extracted text ("Try SampleCo free for 14 days."), and confirm.
  3. Test PDF Extraction: Under Campaign Brief / PDF, upload docs/manual-fixtures/sample-guidelines.pdf. Review the 2-page text preview. Save version and run review checks.
  4. Test Guideline Import: Switch to Manager. In Sources, click "Import guidelines from PDF" with sample-guidelines.pdf. Inspect extracted rule cards and click "Publish source version".
  5. Test Approval & Export: Switch to Reviewer. Notice checks re-evaluated against the new rulebook. Complete manual checkboxes, click "Approve version", and download the signed HTML audit package.
